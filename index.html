About

BECHARA
EL KHOURY

With over 16 years of experience across 30+ countries, I work at the intersection of storytelling, visual craft, and production leadership. As a Director and Cinematographer, I bring emotion and precision to screen, whether for commercials, documentaries, music videos, branded content, or social media.

I also lead creative teams as Head of Media, overseeing concept development, filming, and post-production with a focus on quality and meaningful narratives. From remote nature shoots to high-end studio work, I thrive on creating visuals that resonate deeply and leave lasting impact, while building full media production departments from the ground up.

Beyond Filmmaking

Founder of Leaf and Hook, a certified expert and teacher in cold therapy, breathwork, and nature survival. Operating since 2026 across 15+ countries, leading expeditions and workshops that help people build resilience in extreme environments.

Multi-instrumentalist and creator of Prana Harmony, live music and breathwork journeys that merge sonic atmosphere with physiological transformation.
